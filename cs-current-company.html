Inventing a mobile app for security professionals

CompanyKeep Aware RoleLead UX Designer (sole designer) Timeline2024 – present FocusMobile UX / Security operations · 0-to-1

The problem

Keep Aware's product was desktop-only, but the people using it — SOC analysts and incident responders — don't stop being on-call when they close their laptop. When a high-severity alert fired outside business hours, there was no way to triage or respond without getting to a workstation, and by the time someone did, they were walking in cold.

[ Describe how this problem surfaced — a customer request, a churn signal, a support pattern — and why leadership greenlit exploring a mobile product. ] I was asked to validate whether a mobile companion app made sense for our security-conscious enterprise customers, and if so, take it from concept to a shipped v1 as the sole designer on the project.

Execution

Designing for glanceable, high-stakes decisions

The desktop product was built for depth — dense tables, layered detail views, time to dig in. Mobile forced the opposite: a security professional glancing at a lock screen needs to know, in seconds, whether something requires action right now.
